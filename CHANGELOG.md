# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First release.

- Turns your game sound up while you fish so the bite splash is easy to hear, with options to mute music and ambience. Your own settings come back when you stop fishing.
- Keeps sound playing while you're switched to another window.
- Marks your own bobber with a raid target icon, so you can tell it apart from other players' bobbers.
- Fishing cast bar in the style of the default cast bar, showing the time left on your cast.
- Turns auto loot on while you fish.
- Reminds you when your fishing pole has no lure.
- Settings under Options → AddOns → Obvious Fishing, plus a Credits page.
- `/obvfish` chat command.
